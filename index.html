FAQs

What is the Wellness Coins program?

It is a loyalty initiative that rewards you for shopping and engaging with our wellness brand. Coins can be redeemed for vouchers, products, and member-only services.

How do I join?

Create a free account on this site. After you register, you can sign in anytime to see your balance, earn more coins, and redeem rewards.

Do I get a physical membership card?

Membership is fully digital. Your account is your pass—no plastic card required.

How do coins expire?

Coins are valid for up to one year. We send reminders before any balance expires so you can redeem in time.

Is there a limit to how many coins I can earn?

There is no earning cap. The more you shop and participate, the more you can accumulate.
